À Propos

"Kolo Designz is a leading luxury fashion house based in Douala, Cameroon. Under the creative direction of Chlautille Fobuzie, the brand specializes in bridal wear, haute couture, traditional attire, and corporate looks. Known as one of Africa's premier designers, it blends global acclaim from Serbia Fashion Week, the Annual Show Paris, and FIAFA. Committed to empowering young women through professional training and world-class craftsmanship."
